The fleet is the crew and its rulebook: three departments, a coordinator that proposes, two specialist agents, and the five ERP commands they may issue. A payload is one supplier's case, carried through that fleet for months: onboarded, then renewals, a hold, a release. This page is the rulebook. The fleet runs one mission, the supplier-compliance workflow; the routing policy reads this same catalog, so the page cannot drift from what runs. The number in each cell is how many payloads actually exercised it: see the cases.

Out of scope is refused, and recorded
An event whose department proposes an agent outside its scope, or a command outside its scope, is refused and recorded. Never silently dropped, never silently allowed.
A department is asserted, not authenticated
The department label on an event comes from its producer. This is a policy-and-audit boundary, not a security boundary.
Authority ships with the deployment
Revoking a scope takes a deploy of a new catalog artifact, not a configuration flip.
